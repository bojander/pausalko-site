Paušalko

Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Paušalko is a private invoicing app operated by Bojan Dereta for two users. This page explains what data it keeps and why.

What the app stores

  • Issuer details: business name, address, tax ID (PIB), company number (MB), bank account (IBAN, SWIFT), contact email addresses and the Google account email used to sign in.
  • Client details: name, address, country, VAT number and the email addresses invoices are sent to.
  • Invoices: number, dates, services, amounts, currency and the exchange rate used.

On the device, the app keeps the selected user and the current sign-in token in the app's local storage.

Why

Only to create invoices, generate their PDFs, email them to the clients a user chooses, and show the yearly total against the flat-rate tax limit.

Google user data

  • Google Sign-In: the app receives the signed-in account's email address and uses it only to recognise which of its users is signing in.
  • Gmail (send only): the app uses the gmail.send permission solely to send invoice emails with the invoice PDF attached when a user asks it to. It does not read, search, modify or delete any email, and stores no email content.

Paušalko's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than the above. It is kept with the hosting and database providers that run the app, and invoice emails go only to the recipients a user chooses. Exchange rates are fetched from public National Bank of Serbia data; no personal data is sent for that.

Retention and deletion

Invoices and related records are kept for as long as the law requires invoices to be retained. To ask for access to or deletion of data about you, email the address below. Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
