Paušalko

Invoicing for paušal sole proprietors

Paušalko is a small, private invoicing app used by two Serbian sole proprietors (preduzetnici) taxed at a flat rate. It issues bilingual invoices as PDFs, emails them to clients, converts foreign-currency amounts at the official National Bank of Serbia middle rate, and tracks yearly income against the flat-rate tax limit.

It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

Google account access

The app uses Google Sign-In to identify its two users, and the Gmail API permission to send invoice emails that a user chooses to send. It does not read, modify or delete any email. Details are in the privacy policy.
